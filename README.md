# Chic Children's Boutique

Design a luxury kids' clothing and accessories e-commerce dashboard (main landing/home page) with an elegant, high-end boutique feel — think Bonpoint, Amaia Kids, or Bonton meets a modern SaaS dashboard.

Overall aesthetic:

Soft, muted color palette: ivory, cream, blush pink, sage green, and warm gold/champagne accents — no bright primary "kiddie" colors

Generous white space, refined typography (elegant serif for headings like "Playfair Display" or "Cormorant," clean sans-serif for body like "Inter" or "Jost")

Subtle gold-foil style dividers, thin borders, and delicate line icons instead of bold, cartoonish ones

Soft drop shadows and rounded corners (not too rounded — 8-12px radius) for a premium, tactile feel

Micro-animations on hover (gentle scale/fade transitions) to convey polish

Layout sections:

Header/Nav — logo centered or left, minimal nav links (Shop, Collections, New Arrivals, Gifting, About), search icon, account icon, cart icon with subtle badge

Hero banner — full-width editorial-style image or carousel of a child in a curated outfit, with an elegant tagline overlay and a refined "Shop the Collection" CTA button (outlined, not filled — luxury brands favor subtlety)

Category tiles — circular or soft-square images for categories like "Newborn," "Girls," "Boys," "Accessories," "Shoes," "Gifting" with elegant labels underneath

Featured/New Arrivals carousel — product cards with soft hover states, small "New" or "Limited Edition" tags in a refined script or small caps

Brand story / craftsmanship strip — a horizontal section with a short line about quality, fabric sourcing, or heritage, paired with a lifestyle image

Testimonials or press mentions — quiet, elegant quote cards or logo strip ("As seen in Vogue Bambini")

Newsletter signup — minimal, centered, with an incentive like "Join for early access to new collections"

Footer — organized columns (Customer Care, About, Policies, Social), soft background tone, small logo mark

Interaction details:

Product cards should have a soft zoom-on-hover effect on images

Buttons should feel restrained — thin borders, uppercase small-caps text, generous letter-spacing

Use whitespace strategically to make the layout feel spacious and premium rather than cluttered

Make it fully responsive, with the same elegance carrying through to mobile view.
